## 🚀 Quick 3-Step Start Guide for Non-Technical Users

### Step 1: Log in in 5 seconds
1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox).
2. Go to `http://localhost:5173`.
3. Click the purple **"Owner"** button under **"Demo Quick Fill"**.
4. Click the purple **"Sign In"** button.

### Step 2: Add your Customer or Product
- **To add a Customer**: Click **"Parties"** on the left menu ➔ Click **"+ Add Customer"** ➔ Fill in Name, Phone, and State ➔ Click **Save**.
- **To add a Product**: Click **"Items"** on the left menu ➔ Click **"+ Add Item"** ➔ Enter Item Name, Price, and GST Rate (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, or 28%) ➔ Click **Save**.

### Step 3: Create and Print a GST Bill
1. Click **"Create Invoice"** in the left menu.
2. Select your customer from the dropdown. The app will automatically determine whether it is an **Intra-State (CGST + SGST)** or **Inter-State (IGST)** transaction based on their state.
3. Select the products you want to add and adjust quantities.
4. Click **"Finalize Invoice"**.
5. Click **"Download PDF"** to save or print your clean A4 Tax Invoice!

## 💡 How Tax Calculation Works (Plain English)

- **Same State (e.g., Gujarat to Gujarat)**: The tax is divided into **CGST** (Central Government) and **SGST** (State Government) equally.
- **Different State (e.g., Gujarat to Maharashtra)**: The tax is charged as **IGST** (Integrated Tax).

You don't need a calculator — the application calculates all taxable values, CGST, SGST, IGST, and Grand Totals automatically!
